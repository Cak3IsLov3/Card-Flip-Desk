Import sales from Seller Hub

  1. Open your orders in Seller Hub ↗ (Bestellungen) and choose Download report (Bericht herunterladen) as CSV. Finished reports are under Downloads ↗.
  2. Upload the file here. You check the columns and which card each sale belongs to before anything is saved.
  3. Importing the same file again is safe: order lines that were imported before are skipped.

Tip: put the custom label (SKU) from the listing generator, e.g. CFD-12, on your eBay listings. The import then finds the right card by itself.
